Publication

Exploring the Potential of Conversational Test Suite Based Program Repair on SWE-bench

A. Cheshkov, P. Zadorozhny, R. Levichev, E. Maslov, and R. Franco Jaldin

2024

We evaluated test-feedback-driven repair on 92 selected SWE-bench Lite problems. With oracle function localization, Llama 3.1 patches passed public and hidden tests for 47% of the problems, compared with 34% for repeated generation under the same request budget.

arXiv:2410.04485 ↗

BSc thesis

GFlowNet post-training for text-conditioned molecule generation

2025–2026

I post-trained BioT5+ as a GFlowNet to generate diverse drug-like molecules from natural-language descriptions. The model uses LoRA, a scalar flow head, and the Detailed Balance objective. I compared on-policy, reward-prioritized, and trajectory-balance-residual-prioritized replay. Combining supervised reference trajectories with on-policy rollouts produced the most stable training.
