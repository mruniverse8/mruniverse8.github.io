Competitive programming

I began competing while in school, represented Bolivia at the International Olympiad in Informatics, and later reached the ICPC World Finals. Competitive programming gave me a strong foundation in algorithms, correctness, complexity, and debugging under time limits.

Results

  • ICPC World Finals: Finalist, 2022.
  • Topcoder Open South America Regional: 2nd place, 2019.
  • ICPC South America Regional: 3rd in 2020, 10th in 2019, and 15th in 2018.
  • Bolivia Preliminary Contest: 1st place in 2018, 2019, 2020, and 2025.
  • International Olympiad in Informatics: Participant in 2014, 2015, and 2017.
  • Ibero-American Olympiad in Informatics: Bronze in 2014 and 2015.
  • Bolivian Olympiad in Informatics: Gold in 2016; silver in 2013, 2014, and 2015.

International training camps

I attended competitive-programming camps in Argentina in 2017, 2019, and 2021; Chile in 2018; and Brazil in 2019 and 2020. The camps included advanced algorithm study, difficult practice contests, and work with competitors and coaches from across South America.
