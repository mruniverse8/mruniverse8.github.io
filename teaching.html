Community work

I served on the Scientific Committee of the International Olympiad in Informatics in 2025, contributing to problem setting, testing, and contest design. I have also organized and set problems for the SanSi Cup Bolivia and the 2023 and 2024 individual national programming contests at UMSS. These activities involved more than 200 participants.
